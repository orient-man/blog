I was a guest of Kajetan Duszyński and Piotr Stapp on the [Better Dev Club podcast](https://betterdevclub.pl/episodes/episode-35). 🎙️ It was one of those conversations that's hard to sum up in a single sentence — we could talk about programming and AI endlessly.

A few of the threads we covered:

- 👷 The role of a Principal Software Engineer at Allegro Fintech
- 🛠️ Where craftsmanship in programming lives today
- 🧩 How functional programming relates to Spec-Driven Development
- 🌊 Whether AI-slop has already flooded us — and why reacting only at the PR stage is already too late
- 🤖 On fintech AI agents and how we build them
- 💸 How much freedom you can give LLMs in finance
- ⚖️ Who AI replaces: engineers or the business?
- 🧗 What rock climbing has to do with the situation of juniors
- 🧓 And finally, in full agony-uncle mode, I share what's worth learning today
